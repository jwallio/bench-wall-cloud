Choosing a score

Start with the two recommended scores marked first, then use the others for specific questions. Ensemble means (dashed lines) are smoother than single runs, so they tend to win RMSE and MAE and lose on rain timing; compare ensembles with ensembles, or use CRPS. Click a card to switch the page to that score.

About these scores

  • Model scores come from the dynamical.org forecast scorecard: each model is checked against hourly airport (ASOS) observations at the nearest grid point. Short-range models only reach day 1 or 2.
  • Climatology (a 10-year normal) and persistence ("same as yesterday") are baselines this site scores itself from the same observations.
  • Rain is scored as 6-hour average rates, with "wet" meaning 0.1 mm an hour or more. Ensembles are scored on their mean, except CRPS.
  • Everything updates each morning. Data: dynamical.org, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
